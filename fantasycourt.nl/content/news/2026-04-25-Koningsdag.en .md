## Kingsday
Monday we will be on the King's market in Eindhoven in the Stadswandelpark to promote Fantasy Court. If you want to get a small taste of Fantasy Court please come and find us. There will be our usual shenanigans of jesters knights and archers. Come and test if you can hit some wild life (plushies) or see who will win in a swordfight duel. The weather is predicted to be good so its going to be an amazing day. See you there!
