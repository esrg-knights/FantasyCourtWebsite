## We are almost ready
The gates of Fantasy Court are almost opening. In less than two weeks, the time will finally be here again. The final preparations are in full swing. You can once again visit lots of wonderful stalls, listen to amazing artists, and enjoy a day full of activities. To see everything there is to do, you can look under the programme section on this website. Fantasy Court takes place on Sunday 24 May from 11:00 at the Koeveld. Entry is free and there is something for everyone. So bring all your family and friends, and see you at the court.
